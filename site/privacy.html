Privacy

This site collects one thing: the email address you give the Windows waitlist, if you join it. The Multiprise app itself has no accounts, analytics or telemetry.

Last updated 8 November 2026

The Windows waitlist

  • What is kept: your email address and the time you joined. Nothing else from the form is stored.
  • What it is for: one email telling you the Windows build is ready. No newsletter, no marketing, and the list is never sold or shared.
  • Where it is: a private Vercel Blob store. Only the maintainer can export it.
  • How long: until that email goes out, or until Windows plans are dropped. Then the list is deleted.

Leave the list

Enter the address you used and it is removed straight away. The reply is the same whether or not the address was on the list.

Everything else

  • No cookies, analytics or trackers on this site.
  • Hosting: the site runs on Vercel, which sees your IP address and browser details to serve pages and to rate-limit the waitlist against abuse.
  • Fonts load from Fontshare, which sees the same request details when your browser fetches them.
